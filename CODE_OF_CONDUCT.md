# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of positive behavior include using welcoming language, respecting differing viewpoints, accepting constructive criticism, and focusing on what is best for the community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, trolling, public or private abuse, and publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code. Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository's security contact. Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or take other actions they deem appropriate.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
